Common questions

Is Synthesia better than Colossyan?

Synthesia has a more premium avatar catalog and stronger enterprise integrations. Colossyan is a solid alternative with fast content updates for training workflows.

Which is cheaper for training videos?

Synthesia is cheaper to start with: it starts at $29/mo (Starter), billed monthly, while Colossyan's first paid plan is $59/mo (Professional), billed yearly. Both also have a free plan.

What are the best AI avatar tools for enterprise?

Synthesia, HeyGen, and Colossyan are the top three. Synthesia leads for enterprise scale, HeyGen for avatar realism, and Colossyan for fast-moving training teams.
